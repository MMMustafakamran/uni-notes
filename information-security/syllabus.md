Final Paper Pattern:

The final examination paper will be prepared strictly according to the following pattern:
The paper will be entirely objective and numerical in nature.
All questions will be in multiple-choice (MCQ) format.
Students will be required to solve each numerical question and tick (✓) the correct answer.
The question paper will include questions from every topic of the prescribed syllabus to ensure complete coverage.
Each question will carry equal marks, unless stated otherwise.
No subjective or descriptive questions will be included.
Marks will be awarded only for correctly selected answers.
